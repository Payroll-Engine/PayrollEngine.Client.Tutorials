Social security

For 2026 the employee social security rate is 8.15 % of the gross wage.

Contributions are payable on wages up to the annual contribution ceiling of EUR 84,600 for 2026.

Minimum hourly wage

In 2025 the Demo Wage Board raised the minimum hourly wage to EUR 14.35.

Tax-free allowance

News: the tax-free allowance was raised in 2025 to EUR 12,480.

The allowance is deducted from the annual taxable income before the tax scale is applied. Employers apply one twelfth of the amount in each monthly payroll.

Demo Income Tax Act, section 4(2): The annual tax-free allowance amounts to EUR 12,480.
